How it works

The store needs no stock. Just trust.

1

Tell the shopkeeper what you need: a motorbike, a phone, a solar panel.

2

Store sends the request

The shopkeeper, who already knows you, submits it in the Qistly app.

3

We pay the seller

Buy from any seller. Pick up from the seller or at your corner store.

4

Pay monthly

Pay your installments at the same counter where you shop every day.

Why it works

Banks need a credit history. The shopkeeper already knows who pays.

Corner stores have given credit for years: customers buy groceries on account and settle monthly. Qistly builds on how credit already works, and every repayment builds a record no bureau has.
